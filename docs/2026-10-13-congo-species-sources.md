# Congo species: published parameters and assumptions

These are the source values for the Phase 1 Congo species configs (sub-project 1B): the African leopard, the blue duiker and the water chevrotain.

**Assumptions.** Every figure marked **A** is a game assumption, not a measurement. The reasoning sits beside it.

**Gaps in the sources.**
- Some primary sources were available only as abstracts or through citing works: Hart 2000, Noss, Dubost (duiker and chevrotain papers), and Ray & Sunquist 2001.
- No field measurements of speed, sense range or flight initiation distance exist for these species in dense forest. All such numbers are **A**.
- The ~12 leopards per 100 km² figure is from **Ivindo NP**, Gabon, not Lopé.

## Notes for the simulation

- **Hunting near villages.** Where people hunt, leopards switch to small prey such as blue duiker and porcupine, and grow scarce.
- **Duikers in the diet.** Blue duikers are a small part of the leopard's diet in intact forest, and the main prey only where larger ungulates have been hunted out.
- **Prey in the Phase 1 world.** It has only two prey species, both small, so the leopard's prey list is narrower than nature's. Red duikers, red river hogs and monkeys arrive in later phases.

## Phase 2A (14 October 2026)

Six species join the three of Phase 1 (plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-14-congo-2a-species.md`): bay and Peters's duikers, red river hog, forest buffalo, sitatunga and the African golden cat. Field values and their sources are in `config/species-congo.sources.json`; every value without a source is marked as an assumption there. The leopard's prey now leans toward mid-sized prey, as at Lopé [3],[23]: hogs, red duikers, buffalo calves and sitatunga, with blue duikers and chevrotains less preferred.
